# NativeAgent 0.6.15

Your agent is one living app: every page is a face it keeps current, what it tells you is part of its record, and it runs faster and lighter.

- Every page, one face: Today, Desk, Settings, Memories, Personality, Trust,
  Providers, Connectors, Capabilities, Helpers, Diagnostics, Home and the
  Inbox are each kept current by your agent from the moment it starts and
  drawn the same on the Mac and the iPhone; your agent reads the same page
  you see
- One inbox, one alert: requests, things that went wrong and news live in
  your agent's own record; an alert reaches your phone, the conversation it
  came from, and the Mac when you are at it, each once; answering anywhere
  closes it everywhere; quiet hours hold everything except what went wrong
- Never stuck on a turn: while your agent writes a long step, the working
  card shows what it is writing and how much, and the "no movement" note
  appears only when nothing is arriving
- Talk while it works: a message you send during a turn is read by that
  turn; news for your agent wakes it at once instead of waiting
- Faster and lighter: getting ready to answer takes about half the time it
  did, and a turn's history is served from the provider's cache, so the fresh
  input each turn costs is a small fraction of what it was
- iPhone: a conversation opens at its newest message in about two seconds;
  a message the phone cannot read is set aside instead of making the phone
  read its history again; Desk, Settings, Memories, Personality, Trust and the
  Inbox on the phone are the Mac's pages, and approving from the phone's
  Inbox carries your paired phone's signature
- Quiet when idle: the Mac sends the phone only what changed
- Pictures from any account: image generation draws with whichever signed-in
  account can
