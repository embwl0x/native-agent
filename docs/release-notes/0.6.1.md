# NativeAgent 0.6.1

The app heals itself, connects to any agent on your Mac, and lets your
agent arrange its own window on a sentence. Turns cost fewer rounds, a
stalled reply is cut on the provider's own evidence, and health reports
only wake anyone when they can say what would go wrong.

## Your window, your way

- Say "put Helpers above Agents", "hide Markets and Music", "show me the
  simple view" or "undo that": your agent rearranges the sidebar, the
  rail, the chat column, Today and its own home, and the window changes
  while it answers. The layout is one document it reads and rewrites,
  with the previous one kept for undo; colours, type and materials never
  change. The phone's More page follows.

## Fewer rounds per ask

- Many actions in one call: archive several items, read several pages or
  search for several things at once, each reporting its own result in
  order. A failed call says what to do instead, or to tell you why, rather
  than inviting a retry. Diagnostics shows rounds per turn and the week's
  heaviest asks.

## A body that heals itself

- Agents and coding helpers are reached by a recipe your agent can read
  and rewrite. When one updates and the connection breaks, the app tries
  again, sees what changed, repairs the recipe and resends once, or says
  exactly what changed. Agents reached over A2A are covered the same way.
- Any agent installed on your Mac is found and offered once; connecting
  goes through the usual approval.
- Every health finding has an owner: the app repairs what it can, sign-ins
  and permissions become one ask, your agent is told what it can triage,
  and code findings go to a harness queue that closes itself when healed.
  A reading that is not yet measured, just launched or a count never
  wakes anyone; a warning says what goes wrong if ignored.
- A reply that sends only keep-alives is cut at that provider's own slowest
  first word on record, never a fixed wall, and the message says how long
  it waited and what arrived.
- The daily body report and the heartbeat read the same evidence: crashes,
  loop failures, slow replies, cuts, misses and repairs.

## The mind

- Memory holds memories only; a judgment loop records misses and turns
  standing lessons into growth, ordered through the agent's own voice.
- Every model call says its purpose; heartbeat and provider checks use
  rules, not model calls; the chat's working card reads from one run
  registry across every door.

## Smaller things

- Mail reads check the account they were asked about. Onboarding's
  Continue follows the model you picked however you picked it. Files list
  the workspace root when no path is given. One module log. Light mode's
  first pass: a white plate, a heavier coat, a paper room, a pale haze.
