# NativeAgent 0.6.23

A faster, steadier app. Reading your Mac's apps takes seconds instead of tens
of seconds, your agent knows where things are on your Mac, and the app is
leaner underneath.

## Faster

- Reading Mail, Messages, Notes and Safari takes about two seconds where it
  took up to twenty, and sees current data while those apps are open
- Independent reads in one turn run together instead of one after another
- Your agent's look at a window takes under a second where it took about four
- Reading another desktop app's window is about three times faster
- Memory recall scores faster, with identical results
- Chrome history reads while Chrome is running
- The phone sync reads only what changed

## Your agent's body on your Mac

- Your agent keeps one picture of where things sit on your Mac, built from
  what happens there rather than from repeated looks
- Each action on your Mac settles when its result shows, not after a fixed
  wait. If you use the mouse or keyboard during it, that is noted beside the
  result
- Chrome clicks are confirmed by their target or by the page they open
- Files your agent writes are recognized as its own when they change on disk

## Other agents

- Your agent can talk with Muse in the background: the message is sent, the
  answer read, and follow-ups land in the same chat
- A message your agent queued for another agent can be withdrawn

## The window and the iPhone

- Home, Desk and Today speak in plain words, and each page leads with its
  content
- Cards on the Mac share one look
- A long Markdown reply no longer styles the wrong words
- The iPhone composer sits on one bar at the bottom edge, and the phone opens
  without a fixed pause

## Fixed

- Quit no longer waits on a slow network lookup, and writes in flight finish
  before the app closes
- A reply cut after a blank line no longer stops the next turn with a
  provider error
- After you grant Full Disk Access, the permission card offers Restart again,
  since that grant takes effect at launch
- An image your agent generates is added to Make directly

## Under the hood

- The core is nine modules instead of sixty-one, built with Swift 6.2's
  strict concurrency
- About 1,300 lines of code nothing used are gone

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or newer.
