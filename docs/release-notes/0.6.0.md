# NativeAgent 0.6.0

More of the Mac is readable and usable in a conversation, on a lighter
model too. Your agent can work every page and action in the app within the
access you give it; weather, maps, Photos, Mail, Messages and GitHub answer
directly; Mac actions say what actually happened; and light mode is a room
you can live in.

## One door

- Your agent can read every page and use every action of the app on a
  lighter model. Pages say what can be done there, what each action takes
  and their current version; find follows what you want to do, including
  the difference between reading something, opening it and sending it.
- Previews check a request and its target, say what would be refused or
  need approval, and leave the action undone. A page's version can be
  checked before acting and carried into the next step.
- Long results have a way to get the next part, and plain reads chain in a
  script. Ask about model use to see recorded calls and tokens by model,
  day or chat surface.

## Weather, places and Photos

- Current, hourly and multi-day weather with local units, sunrise and
  sunset. Nearby places around an address or town, with distances and
  contact details. Driving and walking routes with distance, time and key
  directions, and a place partway along the route.
- Photos counts over a date range and recent items' dates, dimensions,
  favourites and stored locations, from library metadata; Photos access is
  requested when first used.

## Mail and Messages

- Large inboxes are read through Mail's own index, and message bodies come
  from their saved copies on disk, HTML-only mail included.
- Search combines sender, date, unread state, category and attachments;
  unread mail reads oldest first; sender counts and unread totals say what
  they cover. Attachments list with names and sizes and save to an allowed
  folder. Asking for a draft keeps it unsent.
- Messages reads sent and received history across conversations, with
  unread counts and oldest-unread ordering.

## Reminders, calendar, GitHub, quotes

- Apple Reminders lists can be created and renamed. Calendar reads
  understand "next week" and "the next N days"; times carry their zone.
- GitHub Actions runs, failed jobs and failed steps; repository search by
  stars or forks; repository names accept GitHub links; adding a tracked
  repository keeps the ones already tracked.
- Stock, index and currency quotes, with common index names, the source and
  the quote time.

## Chrome and web

- Chrome history can be searched by title or address, read by recent visit
  or counted by site, without the extension.
- Page media can be played, paused and seeked, with playback checked and
  the tab named; tab status shows sound and mute state.
- The agent's tabs live in their own group and each conversation remembers
  its last tab; a tab you take becomes yours. Pages keep code, tables and
  links in reading order; PDFs read; a thin page offers a rendered read.

## The Mac

- Actions name the app and control they reached, refuse approximate
  targets, and report a changed target or an unconfirmed result plainly.
- Large app lists read their visible rows; an unresponsive app ends the
  wait with a reason. Background work continues while you use the Mac; a
  small floating chat stays with you while the agent drives it.
- Volume, mute and Now Playing have direct controls. App versions, macOS
  updates, uptime, busy processes, network and disk use read without
  opening their settings screens. Files can be created, copied, moved or
  put in Trash; Notes edited or put in Recently Deleted.

## Senses

- Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers, EPUB and rich text read as pages
  with their paragraphs, tables, slides and chapters.
- During a conversation, sense.make lets your agent make or repair the way
  it reads a file kind, app or site from what it just read, try it, and
  keep it for that place. Senses keep versions and can be rolled back.

## Chat and work

- Work puts Steps, Screen and Make beside the chat; mockups and images keep
  versions. Replies stream as formatted text and settle as selectable prose.
- Queued messages and steering survive a restart; original messages stay
  readable after compaction; archived conversations stay searchable; "this
  chat" means the conversation the request came from.
- The command palette finds conversations, memories, settings and tabs.

## Phone and Telegram

- The iPhone shows the whole shared conversation, live turns from the Mac
  and Telegram included, and older history. Files can be sent from the
  phone and shared cards answered there. Mac settings can be read and
  changed from the phone.
- A Telegram card answered on any surface loses its buttons and shows the
  outcome; approvals reach your direct chat once.

## Look and care

- Light mode is a paper room with frosted plates and a pale haze, not a
  grey one. Larger text and reduced transparency are respected.
- Backups include archived conversations, Senses and Make versions;
  restoring an older backup keeps what it never included.
- Doctor repairs what it can and brings a sign-in or permission need to
  chat. Startup and page changes use less memory.
