# NativeAgent 0.6.14

Mac and iPhone together: your agent now talks, listens and keeps up on the iPhone, and the Mac app underneath got one structure.

- iPhone and Mac together: talk with your agent by voice from the iPhone in a
  call-style mode, with sentence-by-sentence speech and barge-in; hold to talk
  sends a voice note as text; replies stream to the phone as they are written,
  and the first words arrive in about three seconds instead of fifteen
- Phone senses: with your permission the iPhone can tell your agent where you
  are (saved places), whether you are moving, your Focus, charging and
  battery, headphones and today's photo count; each is its own switch on the
  phone, and turning one off never waits on the Mac
- Pairing that holds: a second Mac on the same Apple account can no longer
  answer for the first; pairing reads who actually holds it in iCloud; Check
  iCloud delivery on the pairing page replaces copying a key by hand; the
  Mac's presence recovers on its own after a network drop
- Stop keeps what was said: a stopped reply stays on screen once, saved under
  the same row it streamed into, with Try again under it; a conversation on
  screen is now exactly the saved conversation with the reply being written
  over it, so nothing waits for a refresh
- Repairs that settle themselves: when something fails, your agent owes a
  repair for exactly that thing, and the next time it works the debt clears on
  its own; a stop of a running program is no longer mistaken for its exit; one
  damaged ability file is named on its own instead of hiding the rest
- Trust decides every act: your saved Write switches hold under every
  posture, Full Mac included; a queued Codex task asks Trust again at the moment it launches, so
  lowering Trust governs work already waiting
- One structure: a sweep of about 900 findings across the whole codebase
  removed fallbacks, legacy readers and duplicate paths, so each job has one
  owner and one rule
