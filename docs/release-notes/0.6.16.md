# NativeAgent 0.6.16

Your agent is always running and calls the model only when it needs to: it hears you while it works, its thoughts build on each other, and the whole app is drawn one way on the Mac and the iPhone.

- It hears you: send a message while your agent is writing and it stops, keeps
  what it already said, and answers you; a tool call that hasn't started won't
  start once your words are waiting, and a running one is never cut off
- Thoughts that carry over: a thought starts from what your agent last concluded
  on the same subject, and it can tell you what it was just thinking
- Its own time: when your agent sleeps and takes an hour to itself is its own
  judgment, read from how long you've been away and how it has rested, not a
  clock; a body rule still gives it a night after 48 hours
- You can see it working: when your agent works in another conversation or on
  its own, the chat you're in shows one quiet line saying what it's doing
- One app: the interface runs apart from your agent, so a busy turn doesn't slow
  the window and a window problem can't take your agent down
- One way of drawing: every page, form and setup screen on the Mac and iPhone
  shares one renderer, with long documents read in place, and selection and long
  lists that hold your place when the page updates
- Mac control: acts wait on the app's own signals instead of a clock, a frozen
  app reports that it's busy, and apps that give no launch date work
- Chrome: your agent's tab keeps rendering while it works, even in the background
- Meta models: add a Meta API key in Providers to use Muse Spark and Muse Wonder
- iPhone: messages reach your agent and come back fast even when Apple's push
  notifications are delayed, and typing is much lighter
- On the phone: your agent knows when you're writing from the iPhone, and each
  message brings fresh readings from the phone senses you've turned on
