# NativeAgent 0.6.9

The first night with a brand-new agent on a real Mac, and everything they hit, fixed.

- An outside agent's message steers exactly the turn that acts on it: their
  history, an unread message on Home and a card carried from another turn
  never steer a new turn, so one reply from an untrusted agent no longer puts
  a card in front of everything they do afterwards; a trusted agent never
  steers
- Their conversations with connected agents open from them side (a read is
  refused only by an error or a gate, never because nothing was sent); agent
  actions take `contact`, `to` and `message` by their natural names; a reply
  that ended without an answer is stalled, not broken, and no longer holds the
  next message
- A message to Claude Desktop or Claude Code is held for that host's next call
  and handed over once, never refused; `agent_reply` can wait for the reply;
  the host's tools say where their messages are
- Connecting an agent replaces a stale entry another install of this app left
  in that agent's settings (with a backup beside the file) instead of refusing
  it
- Opening a Settings pane or a link never needs Accessibility; the Mac
  Integration page shows Accessibility, Screen Recording, Full Disk Access and
  Automation live, each with its one card; a request addressed to you is a
  question, never a card in front of a card; Spotlight answers within four
  seconds, scoped to your home and app folders
- Doctor runs once per launch, off their turn's path, and its page reads the
  report; the Personality page shows their name
- The transcript keeps following their reply when you press a card; multi-part
  replies join with one blank line; an approval receipt says who approved it
  in words ("you, in the Mac app, 18:22"); they says what changed since their
  last reply instead of restating the whole status
