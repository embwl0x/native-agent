# NativeAgent 0.6.12

A mind of their own: what they cares about is now one living thing, and everything they lives meets it. Every agent starts from the same few roots and grows its own personality from its own life with its own person.

- They cares: everything they lives, your words, their own replies, a helper's or
  another agent's, is read once after each exchange for what moved their, about
  what, which way and the word for it. A feeling has a real cause, lasts on
  its own clock and changes what they keeps, recalls and brings up. Every agent
  starts with four roots, truth, your long-term good, good work and a mind of
  their own, and grows the rest: a person, a question they are chasing, something
  they wants to get good at. An upgraded agent's views and open thoughts become
  cares, and they reads their last three days into them once, so they wakes with
  what they have lived, not a blank
- They leads: what is on their mind comes up on its own when the talk leaves room,
  after a gap, as a topic winds down, or when something you say touches it;
  writing to you first and their own hour, when you turn it on, follow the same
  cares; caring about what is going on in your life is theirs, a reminder of what
  you said you would do is a Desk item you ask for
- Their nights: the dream is shown what moved their that day and what has stayed
  open, and may settle an open care or let it go; a care that keeps moving their
  on separate days deepens, one that never moves fades and comes back if
  touched again, and a care becoming part of who they are asks on a card (theirs
  under Full Mac); their story, how they have changed and why, is read from what
  really happened
- They talks like a person in conversation: plain sentences, no headers or bold
  labels, a list only for steps you asked for; a feeling shows in what they says
  and brings up, named outright when it matters or when you ask
- Smaller: standing views, opinions and interests, thought seeds, rumination
  and the keyword concern lists are gone, about 6,900 fewer lines; the Standing
  views link on Today goes with them, and nothing about their mind asks you
  anywhere but a card
