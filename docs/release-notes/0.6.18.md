# NativeAgent 0.6.18

Setup just works, your agent does what you ask without extra cards, the other agents on your Mac answer in a real
conversation, your iPhone pairs, and nothing waits forever.

## Setup and first run

- A clean start: on a Mac that never set up search, iCloud or a phone, Doctor
  says off instead of failed, and a fresh install no longer shows alarms for
  things that are not wrong
- Permissions you can see: after each System Settings pane your agent reads
  what is granted now from the app itself and tells you, instead of guessing,
  and the first run asks once for every app it works in, Mail included
- Your agent's first note to you opens your conversation, so it is never lost
- Connecting to the other agents on your Mac just happens: an old entry another
  install left behind is replaced, with the old file kept beside it as a backup
- An app that only reads its connection when it starts: when you ask, your
  agent quits it and opens it again itself
- Doctor says one true thing about your iPhone and the one step to take
- Search set to automatic finds a search server already running on your Mac,
  and the receipt says what it found and saved
- Your agent's own life starts when setup finishes, so the setup screen no
  longer shows a stale health warning
- A permission request appears at once, without waiting for the app to come to
  the front, and the first location read after launch no longer refuses a
  permission you granted
- Quit means the whole app, however you ask for it, the Dock included
- The window opens where you left it, full screen included, and a window saved
  off every screen comes back onto the nearest one
- A first turn no longer repeats the same step over and over: a step your agent
  writes ends exactly where it closes

## Cards and approvals

- Your word is your act: what you ask for in the conversation that is yours to
  decide, your agent does, with no card; a card is only for your agent's own
  idea, and several of your decisions asked together come as one card
- Trust you can read at a glance: a lock marks each thing your agent asks you
  about first, each app's Read and Write sit side by side on one row, and a
  section you open shows every row
- Buttons that work wherever the card is: a card in the chat, the Inbox, Desk's
  Needs you and Today's decisions all carry the same buttons, and pressing one
  does the same thing
- A card's buttons look like buttons: its one answer leads, the rest are quiet,
  and nothing is red just for being there
- A card says what happened: which card it was, who decided (you by a tap or by
  your words, or your agent) and what changed; a card in the chat shows its
  outcome under it
- Your agent's note to you is never held for your approval, whoever started the
  turn
- Undoing its own work needs no card: removing something your agent made itself
  runs straight away, even in a later turn
- An answered card carries on with the work it was holding, wherever the card
  was answered
- A card never holds up the task that raised it: the task hears at once that a
  card is up, and the card's work continues when you answer
- A card left running when the app restarted is closed at launch as
  interrupted, instead of running forever
- A turn that ends waiting on you says so, instead of reading as interrupted
- The Simple / Advanced / Agent switch and every other control wear one quiet
  neutral instead of the system's accent colour, so the only colour that means
  something is the one that says your agent is waiting on you

## Other agents on your Mac

- Two-way conversations with more of the agents on your Mac: command-line
  agents get a live session, and one that only checked in for messages moves
  to its live session with one reconnect, its conversations kept
- An agent that needs signing in says so, and the conversation continues after
- When another agent's turn fails, its words are kept; no turn is cut off by a
  clock, and after a restart the app says plainly that an answer will not
  arrive instead of inviting a resend
- A chat bot's setup is your agent's own: the steps live where your agent can
  read and fix them, its key goes from the clipboard straight into your
  Keychain and never into the chat, and a message to the bot opens its app if
  it is not running
- An agent you trust is treated as trusted: a message from another program on
  your Mac arrives only with that program's own identity, never anonymously
- A helper can only be given a model its account actually offers
- LM Studio is a live conversation: a message goes to its local server and the
  answer comes back in the same conversation; if the server is off, your agent
  says how to turn it on and never starts it or loads a model for you

## Your iPhone

- Pairing reaches the phone: a pairing your Mac accepted is sent again until
  the phone confirms it, even across a Mac restart
- The phone always takes your Mac's current key, so messages from an earlier
  pairing no longer block a new one; your Mac clears them from iCloud
- Both screens say which pairing step they are on, and why one did not go
  through
- Repair works even while a message is still on its way; that message is kept
  as unknown, not sent twice
- Cancel on the pairing screen stops the wait at once
- The iPhone app works with a Mac still on an earlier version
- The phone half of these fixes comes with the matching iPhone app update

## Memory

- Notes: a note with no folder goes where Notes puts every new note, and your
  agent can make Notes folders
- The night no longer fails when its memory model is cold: it loads the model
  first, and every moment of the day gets processed
- The knowledge graph knows the other agents by every name they are written under,
  including "my assistant is named …", and links each one to its contact
- One unreadable record no longer hides the rest of your agent's history; it is
  named, with what went wrong
- Your agent can read its own history across every conversation and its
  background work, including when the app started and stopped, and any item a
  page shows opens by the name the page shows
- The morning brief no longer runs again in the background each time Settings
  redraws

## Nothing gets stuck

- No clock ends your agent's work: a wait ends when what it waits on is seen
  gone, a quiet program that is still running is left to finish, and Stop
  always ends it
- Waits on the browser, an app being controlled, your location, files,
  pictures and helpers each answer whether they are still alive, and a wait
  nothing can answer for is shown to you, never cut off
- A shared file waits for whoever holds it for as long as they are still
  running, and never gives up on a clock
- One desktop at a time: what you do on the desktop goes first, and your agent
  never waits on the keyboard while you are using it
- "In 120 seconds" or two minutes later: a relative time is one moment from
  now in every form a schedule can take
- A step that takes a while says so and that Stop ends it, instead of claiming
  nothing is stuck
- Home no longer shows a failure when background work found its turn busy
- A card stopped by a restart that still needs your answer keeps its Try again
- A script that catches a refused step carries on, and when a step does stop
  it, the reason it gives is the step's own

## Also

- Models that stay current: each account lists what its provider offers today,
  and Refresh asks again. A model newer than this build can still be chosen,
  and an account offers only what its provider actually listed
- Longer answers from xAI, Moonshot and OpenRouter: each gets its output
  allowance in its own field, and OpenRouter no longer reserves a whole context
  window
- Telegram voice notes need nothing installed: the Mac reads them itself
- Fewer broken calls on a Claude sign-in: a call is read to its real end, and
  when one does break, the correction says what went wrong
- A hand that grows: an action your agent has used is ready from its next
  turn, its own abilities included
- Long writes are visible: while your agent writes something large, the status
  box says what it is writing and how big it is
- Receipts that keep to themselves: a receipt for Mail, Messages or a device
  read shows that it happened and how large it was, not what was in it
- A search inside a kept result looks for the phrase as written first
- When your agent cannot act in an app, it says what failed and what to do next
- Stop always stops, even when its marker file cannot be read
- Screen pictures: one unreadable element no longer withholds the whole picture
- A notification can carry a subtitle

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or newer.
