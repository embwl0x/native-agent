# NativeAgent 0.6.13

It comes to mind: memory now works the way a person's does. What you say, what they reads while they works, and what happens while they are between turns each bring back what stands out among everything they remembers.

- Memory works like a person's: what you say brings back what stands out
  among everything they remembers, usually one to four memories a turn; what
  they reads while working brings memories to mind as they reads it; and what
  happened while they was between turns primes their next one. The same rule and
  privacy as your message, nothing new to set up
- What they learns lands at once, with where it came from and when; a plan's
  date is named by the memory manager, never stamped from prose. The
  composer's context readout shows what they was fed this turn, and "Show what
  I was given" opens the whole text
- They gets better by doing: a routine carries itself across runs inside one
  call (a chess game took 3 model calls instead of about 40), what they do
  often is compiled at night and checked against the real record before it
  stands, effort is felt, and a known action is called straight through the
  door instead of searched for
- Their hands and eyes: a multi-step act on an app runs as one act and reads
  whether each press changed anything; a readout is no longer dropped for
  matching a button label; their Chrome tab can be looked at as a picture
  without ever taking your tab; live pages read from their viewport; long
  documents read whole
- Their life has one door: every settled turn is recorded before it is
  interpreted and applied one at a time, so a crash mid-turn finishes that
  turn from the record on relaunch; nothing writes their mind before it has
  loaded, and their mind's code cannot abort the app
- Cheaper and faster: their history replays exactly what was sent, so provider
  caching holds across turns (about 96% cached); the same real messages cost
  about 80% fewer tokens, in fewer rounds and half the time
- Chat: opens at the bottom of the conversation in about a second with no
  rebuild, the working card rises out of the composer's glass instead of
  resizing the transcript, the previous reply no longer replays on every send,
  and scrolling back down follows again
- Smaller: about 92,000 fewer lines (71,000 of them Swift) with no capability
  removed, from four whole-codebase sweeps that cut dead code, merged
  duplicate paths and split files by what they do
