# NativeAgent 0.6.8

The body reports, the agent decides: when something cannot be done, they hears
why and answers, instead of the app ending their turn for them.

- Their turn has one ending, their own. A slow provider start, a spent helper
  budget, a card waiting on you, a full context, a failed compaction or a
  window that stopped listening all reach the agent as receipts they answers;
  their words appear beside a card, and the card still gates the action.
- Stop always stops, on every door (Mac, iPhone, Telegram, Slack, connected
  agents). Closing a window only hides the reply, and relaunching the app
  resumes an accepted turn from where it was, with its finished words intact.
- A forgotten or tightened memory never comes back: the memories about to be
  spoken are checked against the store at the moment they are chosen, and
  again if one is expanded. Recall and saving never wait on the embedding
  model or on background tidying, and launch reopens the last context at once.
- Permission is one decision: your posture (Full Mac, Work, Safe) and what the
  tool declares it does. Per-tool saved permissions, MCP consent gates, "not
  in scripts" flags, word lists and payload scans are gone; Full Mac decides
  exactly as before. A request steered by an untrusted connected agent may
  read; anything else waits for you.
- A connected agent you trust is trusted by its door, never by its name:
  reading a trusted agent's messages and then sending one no longer raises a
  card.
- Providers: one source of truth for what each model can do (tools, images,
  effort, context window). Key states say what was proven: saved, checked,
  rejected at its last check, or could not check (an outage is not a rejected
  key). A context overflow says the context is full and names the lever.
- One reply through every door: Telegram carries their attachments and no longer
  reads your words for model or effort phrases; the iPhone keeps their words on
  stop or error, and its Stop and Steer reach the running turn.
- Anything for you is owed until received: a delivery that fails on one door
  stays visible as owed, never dropped or silently rerouted, and a connected
  agent's callback survives a relaunch.
- The app never says "nothing due" over damage: unreadable scheduler state,
  run history or journals show as faults with their file and fix, and timing
  it cannot read is unknown, never "fresh".
- The shell asks the one permission law before running, the Mac's time zone
  rules every date, and iCloud Drive work runs off the turn's path (a sync
  scan that once stalled a turn for eleven minutes now reads metadata only).
- Smaller: about 2,100 fewer lines.
