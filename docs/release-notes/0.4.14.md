# NativeAgent 0.4.14

The chat box gets out of the way, the agent introduces itself on a fresh
install, and other agents that connect meet the whole agent, memory and all.
Plus a long list of fixes from two full passes over the tree.

## Chat composer

- **One field, one row.** The old settings popover, capabilities pill and
  Context receipt are gone. Under the field sit three words: the model, the
  thinking level, and Trust. Each opens a small card above its word; the
  conversation never moves.
- **Providers first.** The model card lists your connected providers. Hover
  one and its models fly out beside the card and scroll on their own, so a
  provider with hundreds of models stays usable. Fast lives inside the model
  card and only shows on the row while it is on.
- **A context ring** on the row shows how much of the conversation's context
  is used; hover it for the exact tokens against the model's window.
- Keyboard: Tab from the draft into the words, arrows and number keys inside a
  card, Escape closes it from anywhere in it.

## Onboarding

- Setup asks your name and the agent's name, as before. Then, on a fresh
  install, the agent opens the conversation itself: "Hi, I'm here on your Mac.
  What do you want me to be for you?" A partner, a friend, a coworker, an
  assistant, in your own words. Your answer becomes one line in its own
  persona, shown as a receipt beside the reply. Skip it and it simply gets to
  work.
- A greeting that could not be sent (no working provider yet) retries on the
  next launch instead of being lost.

## Agents talking to agents

- Another agent that connects over A2A, MCP, or the bundled helper meets the
  whole agent: its personality, memories, recall and full tool set, in its own
  conversation. The agent's own notes from those conversations carry who said
  what.
- Anything a peer asks the agent to change (files, shell, sends, its own
  persona) raises your normal permission card, with the peer named. Trust a
  peer under Trust → Connected agents and it runs as the agent's own action.
- Approval cards and the peer's conversation row show the peer's name.

## Memory and growth

- Conversation recall takes date bounds, oldest-first or newest-first order,
  and can search tool receipts on their own.
- Growth entries stay one short line each, including proposals held over
  from earlier passes.

## Trust and safety

- Connected agents have their own credentials; a peer's conversations live
  under its own namespace and cannot land in yours.
- Bounded inbound reads on the bridge and Mac sync; replay protection that
  fails closed; file actions verified by descriptor before use.

## Fixes

- Composer clicks, scrolling, keyboard navigation and Shift-Return work
  reliably; a hidden card no longer swallows scrolling on other pages.
- A saved provider default the provider no longer offers is not treated as a
  choice; the surface falls back to the catalog instead of failing.
- Native tool schemas refresh on the next accepted turn after an upgrade.
- The installer stops only the app it is replacing.
- iPhone: an old Mac record the phone cannot verify is archived instead of
  raising "Could not verify one Mac reply" on every launch.
- The release bundle now includes the agent-link helper.
