# NativeAgent 0.5.1

A cleanup release after 0.5.0: the chat keeps its room, nothing pins itself
over your conversation, and typing while your agent works shows up right away.

## Chat

- Messages you send while your agent is working show up at once as the next
  message, with Steer and Remove, instead of disappearing until the turn ends.
- The header is a slim bar, and the strip above the chat only takes the room
  of what is actually waiting. Answered cards leave it.
- Notes above the chat have Clear (marks them read) and Hide, which stays
  hidden until you bring the notes back.
- Approval cards are shorter: a title, one line of why, and the details folded.
  Cards for sending something show who it goes to on the front.
- A card that was already answered or removed no longer leaves an error line.
- Internal notes about delegated work fold into one line instead of appearing
  as if you wrote them.

## Your agent

- Under Full Mac your agent keeps control of the Mac across restarts and
  updates. Using the Mac yourself still stops whatever it is doing in that
  moment.
- Its own decisions, like what goes in its studio canon, stay its own and no
  longer show up as your approvals.
- Lessons from its dream cycle show the lesson itself on the card.

## Phone and Telegram

- Replies to messages you send from the Mac still reach Telegram and the phone
  app, silently, so your phone doesn't buzz while you're at the Mac.
- The phone gets your providers and model settings reliably after a launch.
