# NativeAgent 0.6.7

Every agent is itself, every no names its fix, and the iPhone follows one Mac.

- Other agents on your Mac are contacts under their own names (Claude Code,
  Codex) until you rename them: ask the agent to call one something else and
  it does. Each of an agent's ways in has its own switch in Trust, and only a
  call made with that door's own key carries what you allowed for it.
- Claude Code can be messaged like Codex, through its own command line, with
  your Full Mac or restricted choice carried as its permission flags.
- When the agent can't do something, it says who can fix it and how, and shows
  it as OWED on Home until it works again or it lets it go with a reason.
- A missing macOS permission (Accessibility, Screen Recording and the rest) is
  one card the agent raises itself: it brings up the system prompt and the
  exact Settings pane, and the waiting work carries on when you allow it.
- The agent can set Chrome up itself (chrome.setup): it opens Chrome's
  extensions page and loads the NativeAgent extension. After that the
  extension updates itself when the app ships a newer one.
- "Compacting this chat…" shows while a reply waits on compaction, and the
  background tidy-up never makes a reply wait.
- The agent answers within seconds of launch however much history it has, and
  search across past chats is fast.
- The transcript keeps your reading place, a reply lands in place instead of
  blinking out at the end, and a click in the composer no longer moves the
  cursor.
- The iPhone follows one Mac: a second Mac on the same Apple Account no longer
  takes the phone. Connectors › iPhone, Doctor and the phone all show one
  connection check (pairing, iCloud, last exchange) that says what is wrong
  and who can fix it. A phone reply shows once, and the phone no longer
  flashes an iCloud signature mismatch after a re-pair.
- Two NativeAgent installs on one Mac each keep their own connection in other
  agents' settings.

Pairs with NativeAgent for iPhone 0.6.7.
