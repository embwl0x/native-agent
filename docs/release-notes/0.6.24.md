# NativeAgent 0.6.24

A simpler window, one Table you share with your agent on the Mac and the
iPhone, and an app that tells the truth when you press Stop.

## Four everyday places

- Simple shows four places: Together, Table, your agent's own place under its
  name, and Control, on the Mac and the iPhone. Advanced keeps Chat, Desk and
  Diagnostics
- Command-K finds every page, including the ones Simple tucks away
- The menu bar shows one line when something is waiting on you, and nothing
  when nothing is

## The Table

- The Table is one canvas on the Mac and the iPhone, with the same gestures,
  editing, VoiceOver order and keyboard navigation
- From your paired iPhone you can add, cross out and remove things on the
  Table
- Your agent places things beside, after, under or inside a named item, and a
  container grows to hold what is put in it
- A page can show a slice of the Table, and a Desk item shows its own card
  with what it connects to

## A turn you can watch

- A running turn shows its steps above the composer. From there you can
  withdraw steps that have not started, or stop
- A click or tap acts only on the page you saw. If the page changed
  underneath, it is refused and you are asked to look again
- Dragging the window's edge is smoother

## Other agents

- Your agent keeps a job it handed to another agent in mind for as long as it
  is out, and Today shows who it is waiting on and since when
- One message is one message: a send does not go out twice when two of your
  agent's conversations reach for the same one
- One contact that cannot be read no longer stops your agent's other
  conversations from resuming

## Stop and Quit

- A Stop or a Quit is recorded as a stop. It is no longer filed as a failure,
  a crash or a repair in your agent's actions on your Mac, its scripts,
  calendar and reminder writes, searches, exports, dreams, or the Telegram and
  Slack connections
- A Telegram message that arrives during a Stop or a Quit is kept and answered
  afterwards

## Memory

- A memory can say when it was true. Your agent does not offer a fact that has
  ended, or one that has not started, as current
- A corrected memory points to the one that replaced it
- A night's work carries on past one bad item, and a day without conversation
  still gets its night

## Your agent on your Mac

- Your agent can read what an installed app offers without opening it: its
  document types, URL schemes, scripting dictionary and App Intents
- What your agent builds for itself can read accessibility elements, with
  secure fields masked, open a link in an app, and hold a live connection, all
  inside the access you have already given
- Permission belongs to the request being served. A later request does not
  inherit an earlier one's approval

## Fixed

- Setup no longer stalls after a sign-in that lists no models
- A credential saved in Settings is reported as saved even when the read that
  follows it fails
- The account sheet's Default model sets the Chat model
- Settings actions show their own message, such as a cancelled sign-in
- Waking your Mac no longer stops activity history until the next launch
- The Trust dot, the Fast pill, the model word and More models open their
  Settings pages
- The transcript, the agent pane and document text follow macOS Text Size
- A document opened on the Mac is no longer sent twice
- Doctor heals a damaged search index and lists failing checks first
- A new chat with nothing in it no longer says its transcript is missing
- A permission card for Mail, Messages, Notes or Music no longer gets stuck when that app is not open

## Under the hood

- Seven actions nothing used, the old task ledger and several thousand lines
  of unused code are gone
- New guides: Getting Started, Settings and Where Things Are

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or newer.
