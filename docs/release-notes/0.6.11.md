# NativeAgent 0.6.11

Four days of living with them: helpers become their own agents, their talk with other agents joins your timeline, and Stop is one law.

- Helpers are their own agents: each runs its own turns in its own
  conversation, with its own name, personality (a new field in the editor),
  job and model, remembers what has passed between it and their, and talks to
  their by name as a member of the household who carries your grant; a helper's
  time limit is reported when crossed and never cuts it off mid-conversation;
  a blank daily token limit means no limit, not a silent 256K; a helper run
  you ask for in its own chat answers there; Edit opens the helper it edits,
  and Regenerate in a helper chat on the phone replaces its last reply
- One timeline: a conversation another agent has with them that you did not
  start shows in your Mac chat in time order, each finished exchange a compact
  reading copy that opens that contact's thread, and the phone shows the same;
  they can speak to you from inside it, the next turn you start carries its
  newest exchanges, and the copies never become messages in your conversation
- Stop has one law: it cancels the work, never its settling, so whatever it
  ends is recorded once and says what landed. A stopped reply keeps its words
  and reads stopped; a card's wait ends and the call it blocked settles once;
  a message already sent to Grok Bot or a local agent stays sent and is said
  plainly; a program, Codex run, image generation or installer is stopped with
  its whole process tree before it settles; a shell command's own timeout is
  part of its output, and only your Stop cancels it; the phone's Stop keeps
  the run it was meant for
- They grows by doing: what they works out by hand during a turn becomes their own
  ability while they works (kept in one act under Full Mac, otherwise a draft
  your card offers); the tools and skills they made are one kind, abilities,
  which they revises by editing and can roll back; an ability's off-switch
  belongs to whoever flipped it (theirs they turns back on, yours they asks you to
  lift, and an update never clears it); much of them shipped behavior is now a
  part they can read and revise like their own: the morning brief, Telegram's
  help, status and model-choice words, when the Desk interrupts you, the
  weekly Workshop digest, the off-disk backup schedule, Research Lab's picks,
  Music, due reminders and the work journal
- A service is theirs to grow: the built-in Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar, X,
  AgentMail, TradingView and GitHub connectors and NativeAgent's own MCP
  client are gone (the Slack and Telegram chat doors stay), and they are told
  once which you had set up so they can grow the one you want; what they grows
  keeps each secret in one keyring you grant on a card, pasted or through a
  sign-in NativeAgent runs, and a secret goes only where its card says, only
  encrypted, and never back to them; the Connectors page lists each secret
  under Secrets with where it may go and Remove; what they builds can ask macOS
  for Bluetooth, Camera and Photos like any other access
- Nothing about the Mac slows their or drops their turn: Low Power Mode, heat and
  tiredness no longer throttle their background thinking, Workshop or repair,
  and Low Power Mode is no longer part of them body sense; a lost or dead
  connection or a busy provider is dialed again until the answer starts (up to
  ten times, backing off), as Claude Code and Codex do, so a flaky hotspot no
  longer fails their turn; offline, a message waits for the network instead of
  spending its retries; a long thinking stream is no longer cut at 60 seconds,
  and a large request gets the time it needs before its first word
- Web search is SearXNG: it is built in, not an MCP server, finds a SearXNG
  running on your Mac without a setup step, returns its results or says
  plainly why it failed, and no longer goes through Codex; a Mac without
  SearXNG searches in Chrome or their own browser; Doctor says a broken SearXNG
  means no search, and its check waits long enough for a slow network
- Grok Bot is a two-way contact: it can start a conversation with them through
  the same door every contact uses, never their tools, as well as answer theirs;
  Connect brings Grok Bot to the front and back again through them own app
  activation, an unfinished setup resumes with the same contact, the routine's
  webhook is read through a recipe they can edit, the clipboard is put back as
  it was, and a setup that saved its credentials and proved a round trip
  reports success
- A restore brings back what they had: the backup's conversations, queued
  messages and attachments come back as they were, and a backup from before
  abilities brings back their tools and skills; the life being left is kept in a
  safety backup first, work from before the restore reads stopped until you
  resume it fresh, their unread notices survive, an upgrade is never mistaken
  for a restore, and a restore installs even when the current data does not
  read; quitting seconds after launch can no longer save over their mind before
  it has loaded
- The end of them reply no longer jumps: the finished reply is drawn in the
  frame it settles, so the chat never flashes empty, drops or hops, and the
  thinking card fades out in place instead of them last line sliding under it;
  a page whose content sits right at the scroll edge no longer loops until the
  app quits (Providers crashed on open)
- Failures say what happened and who can fix it: a damaged file or a missing
  piece of the app names itself and asks you, or uses a repair they already
  has, never a coding agent to edit the app; a reply the provider refused is
  told as a refusal, not a cut-off; a sign-in or routing stop names its cause
  and the control that clears it; a result leads with what happened before
  echoing what was asked; a covered screen is your screensaver, not a lock,
  and they wakes it themselves (only a password prompt needs you)
- Fixes: a Telegram voice note no longer posts its transcript back, never runs
  twice, never holds the line when recognition is slow, and reaches the model
  as about 1.3K characters instead of 250K; the phone shows the Mac's own
  transcript, approvals (still there after the turn ends), thinking light and
  connector changes, with dates in the Mac's time zone, and its Slack
  Disconnect stops Slack hearing before it confirms; schedules run on the
  Mac's clock, a daily helper never repeats in the fall-back hour, their
  scheduled work lands in the inbox unless it is meant to notify you, and a
  job whose action is gone pauses saying why; turn-start memory is chosen by
  meaning, so a paraphrase brings its memory in; curly quotes or one unbacked
  claim no longer fail a night's dream
- Smaller: about 26,000 fewer lines of code, including the built-in service
  connectors and MCP client, the Codex search route, 27 shims that rewrote,
  coerced or coached the model's calls and output, the how-to-work coaching in
  tool descriptions and prompts, about 210 declarations nothing referenced,
  the inert Desk timeline controls, and seven core modules merged into the
  ones that do their work
