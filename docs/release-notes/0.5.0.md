# NativeAgent 0.5.0

One brain behind one door. Your agent now uses a single tool to read, do,
find and script across the whole app, and grows skills along the way.
Personality runs on what actually happened between you, not on system noise.
Verified agents enabled in Trust skip extra peer approvals; ordinary Trust
checks and protected exceptions still apply. Required approvals pop up right
in your chat. Three full hardening passes went over every part of the app
before this release.

## One door

- Your agent works the whole app through one always-on tool: read any page,
  do any action, find anything by intent, or run a short script that chains
  steps in one go. Fewer round trips, faster replies, lower cost.
- Full Mac provides persistent full autonomy for ordinary checks. Explicit
  blocks and protected exceptions remain: macOS privacy permission resets
  still need you, as do deletes, sends in your name, persona edits and
  protected approvals when an untrusted agent steered the turn.

## Skills that grow

- Repeatable work becomes a skill the agent writes. A script skill runs in
  one step, checks its own results, and hands back any step needing separate
  action before it runs.
- Skills and authored tools share one lifecycle: add, upgrade (the previous
  version is kept for one-click rollback), and retire. Anything unused for 30
  days is archived, never deleted, and comes back with one call.
- Repeated work can prompt a quiet skill suggestion (at most once a day);
  a skill that keeps stopping at the same step suggests its own upgrade. All
  of this runs on your Mac, costs no model tokens, and its storage is capped.

## Trust and approvals

- Switch a verified agent on in Trust and its authenticated turns carry your
  authority, skipping extra peer approvals. Ordinary Trust and domain checks
  still apply, including explicit blocks and owner approval for macOS privacy
  permission resets even under Full Mac. Trust comes only from identities the
  app verifies itself.
- Every approval your agent needs appears as a card in your chat, on the Mac
  and the iPhone, with buttons in your Telegram chat too. Cards raised in a
  conversation you aren't watching are brought to the one you are.

## Personality

- Each turn the agent gets one well-chosen feeling, or none, instead of a list,
  and inner state stays private and lean.
- Last night's dream can surface as a passing thought when today connects to
  it, always as a dream, never as something that happened.
- After a real break, the agent knows what happened while you were apart,
  including what the agents you work with shipped.
- Lessons keep the moment that taught them, and personal memories have their
  own lane so work rules don't crowd them out.
- Your agent can hold opinions with reasons, change them on evidence, and
  return to open interests.
- With something worth sharing, your agent can reach out first: at most once
  a day, never during quiet hours, and never chasing a reply.
- Your agent can see why a memory or feeling came up, reject a bad association,
  and undo one change.

## Agents

- The agents list tells the truth: Ready means the agent answered its health
  check, waiting ends, and the line never quotes old replies as status.

## Hardening

- Three complete passes over every part of the app fixed secret leaks into
  logs and notifications, crash traps, missing provenance on agent-written
  text, permission gaps, and status messages that claimed things had happened
  when they hadn't.
- A signed-in account is no longer reported as "access expired" just because
  its short-lived token is due to renew.
