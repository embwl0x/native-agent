# NativeAgent 0.5.0

One brain behind one door. Your agent now uses a single tool to read, do,
find and script across the whole app, and they grows their own skills as they
works. Their personality runs on what actually happened between you, not on
system noise. Agents you trust need no approvals, and every approval they do
need pops up right in your chat. Three full hardening passes went over every
part of the app before this release.

## One door

- Your agent works the whole app through one always-on tool: read any page,
  do any action, find anything by intent, or run a short script that chains
  steps in one go. Fewer round trips, faster replies, lower cost.
- Under Full Mac they acts on them own; deletes, sends in your name, persona
  edits and approvals still come to you when an untrusted agent steered the
  turn.

## Skills that grow

- Repeatable work becomes a skill they writes themselves. A script skill runs in
  one step, checks its own results, and hands any step it shouldn't take
  alone back to them before it runs.
- Skills and their own tools share one lifecycle: add, upgrade (the previous
  version is kept for one-click rollback), and retire. Anything unused for 30
  days is archived, never deleted, and comes back with one call.
- They notices repeated work and quietly suggests a skill (at most once a day);
  a skill that keeps stopping at the same step suggests its own upgrade. All
  of this runs on your Mac, costs no model tokens, and its storage is capped.

## Trust and approvals

- Switch an agent on in Trust and it counts as you: nothing it asks for needs
  your approval. Trust comes only from identities the app verifies itself.
- Every approval your agent needs appears as a card in your chat, on the Mac
  and the iPhone, with buttons in your Telegram chat too. Cards raised in a
  conversation you aren't watching are brought to the one you are.

## Their own person

- Each turn they gets one well-chosen feeling, or none, instead of a list, and
  their inner state stays private and lean.
- Last night's dream can surface as a passing thought when today connects to
  it, always as a dream, never as something that happened.
- After a real break, they knows what happened while you were apart, including
  what the agents you work with shipped.
- Lessons keep the moment that taught them, and personal memories have their
  own lane so work rules don't crowd them out.
- They can hold opinions with reasons, change them on evidence, and come back
  to things they are curious about.
- When they have something worth sharing, they can reach out first: at most once
  a day, never during quiet hours, and never chasing a reply.
- They can see why a memory or feeling came up, reject a bad association, and
  undo one change.

## Agents

- The agents list tells the truth: Ready means the agent answered its health
  check, waiting ends, and the line never quotes old replies as status.

## Hardening

- Three complete passes over every part of the app fixed secret leaks into
  logs and notifications, crash traps, missing provenance on agent-written
  text, permission gaps, and status messages that claimed things had happened
  when they hadn't.
- A signed-in account is no longer reported as "access expired" just because
  its short-lived token is due to renew.
