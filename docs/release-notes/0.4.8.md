# NativeAgent 0.4.8

A follow-up to 0.4.7: the memory model is back inside the download, and the Mac and iPhone pairing is tightened.

## The download

- The large memory model ships inside the app again, so one download has
  everything and nothing is fetched on first launch.

## Mac and iPhone

- Every record the Mac sends the phone is now signed and checked the same way
  on both sides, and that agreement is tested so it cannot drift.
- A record the phone cannot verify is set aside for the session instead of
  nagging: one notice, then it is listed under Diagnostics with what it was and
  why, and checked again on the next launch or after re-pairing.
- Stale or reflected records can no longer claim a message's identity.
- The Desk card and pairing steps on the phone are easier to read; pairing
  names the app's own Settings on your Mac.

## Bridge

- When an attachment cannot be read, the notice names the file and says the
  sender can resend it.
