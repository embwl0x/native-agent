# NativeAgent 0.6.20

A table you and your agent share, an agent that wakes for what it planned, and a
quieter Mac when nobody is talking.

## The Desk's table

- The Desk has one table that is always there. You and your agent put notes,
  shapes, arrows, pen strokes and live cards on it
- A card on the table always shows its Desk item's current title and status.
  Crossing it off closes the item, and closing the item crosses the card
- Pop the table out into its own window. It remembers its size and place, and
  keeps working when the main window is closed
- Draw with the pen. Your agent can read what a loop circles and what a line
  crosses, and a circle around a card no longer takes the card's click
- Notes land where you click, grow to fit their words and say who wrote them.
  Boards open fitted, and stay where you moved them until you press Fit
- Any saved agent can read the table; a trusted agent can also write on it, and
  its marks are signed

## Your agent keeps its own plans

- A step your agent sets for itself wakes it at that time to do the work,
  instead of only posting words into your chat. If the turn fails, it is
  offered again
- Your agent can schedule something "in N minutes", and deferring a step moves
  its time
- Changes on your phone and Mac (charging, Focus, network, arriving or leaving
  a place) reach your agent when they happen

## Other agents

- A conversation with another agent reads like a conversation: its name in the
  header, each speaker on their own side
- A message sent to another agent is tracked across relaunch; a delivered
  message says delivered, and a pending reply says waiting
- Coding agents connected over ACP stay connected while idle and restart on
  the next message if their program quit
- Telegram: in an allowed group only allowed senders are trusted, and voice
  messages are transcribed on the Mac

## Memory and the night

- The nightly dream reads the oldest conversations first and never stalls on a
  very long one; what did not fit is read the next night
- When memory is full, older memories are archived instead of deleted, and
  your corrections are kept longest
- Recall can come back empty when nothing is related, and an old memory is no
  longer buried just for being old

## Mac control

- Saving through a Save panel is confirmed from what the panel reports
- Menu commands work in apps behind other windows; Chrome history can be read
  while Chrome runs; Gmail's Sent and Drafts are found
- Editing or deleting one occurrence of a repeating calendar event changes only
  that occurrence

## Quieter and steadier

- With nobody talking, background work runs only when something changed, and
  chat animations pause while Chat is hidden
- Fixed a crash after long uptime
- Stop during a sign-in refresh no longer signs your agent out of its provider
- Quit now also finishes when the background process does not answer
- Model lists refresh themselves daily; a saved list stays usable if a refresh
  fails
- Web fetches cannot be redirected into your Mac or local network

## iPhone

- The lock screen reads "Working on your message" instead of showing your
  message

## Removed

- The scheduled morning brief and its Settings controls. Your agent can still
  put a brief together as a step of its own
- Scan findings no longer arrive as Inbox cards; they go to your agent, which
  brings you what matters

## Correction to 0.6.19

- The Watches line in the 0.6.19 notes described behaviour that ships in this
  release, not in 0.6.19

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or newer.
