NativeAgent is the iPhone and iPad companion for your NativeAgent on Mac.

Continue conversations, review activity, manage approvals, inspect memories,
use Workshop, and stay connected to your agent away from the Mac. Communication
between the companion and its paired Mac uses authenticated Apple iCloud
transport, while the Mac remains the authority for tools, trust, and actions.

NativeAgent requires a compatible NativeAgent installation on a Mac signed in
to the same iCloud account.
