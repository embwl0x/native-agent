NativeAgent 0.4.6 brings the iPhone and iPad companion up to date with the latest Mac release.

- More reliable chat: new conversations stay in their own sessions, replies appear in the right chat, and older sync updates no longer replace newer history.
- Better recovery when connections drop, including saved message queues and safer retries that avoid duplicate actions.
- Improved notification routing, approval handling, and connection status.
- More dependable provider and model controls, photo attachments, and voice dictation.
- Accessibility and navigation improvements across Chat, Activity, and the synced Desk.

Requires NativeAgent on a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Update the Mac companion to 0.4.6 for the latest capabilities.
