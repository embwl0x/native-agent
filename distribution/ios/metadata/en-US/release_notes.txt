NativeAgent 0.6.11 for iPhone and iPad goes with NativeAgent 0.6.11 on your Mac.

- Reads everything a current Mac shares: approvals, inbox, memories, providers and chat open again instead of reporting a damaged copy.
- Approvals, inbox, memory decisions and model choices show exactly what the Mac decided, and stay settled once you answer them.
- Stop and Steer from your phone reach the same conversation on the Mac.
- Chat reads one transcript with the Mac, attachments and inline cards included, and stays at the bottom while a reply types out smoothly to its end.
- Voice: see every word as it's heard, and tap Send once to stop and send.
- Schedules and reminders follow your Mac's clock and time zone.

Requires NativeAgent on a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.
