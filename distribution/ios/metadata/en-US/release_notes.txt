NativeAgent 0.6.13 for iPhone and iPad goes with NativeAgent 0.6.13 on your Mac.

- Reads everything a 0.6.13 Mac shares: chat, approvals, inbox, memories and models.
- Only a decision still owed sits at the bottom of the chat; a request that already finished, failed or expired stays with its turn and in Activity.
- Approvals you answer on the phone settle through the same signed path as the Mac, and the card shows what happened.
- Leaner: much of the phone's code was simplified with nothing taken away.

Requires NativeAgent on a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.
