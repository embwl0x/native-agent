NativeAgent 0.6.7 for iPhone and iPad goes with NativeAgent 0.6.7 on your Mac.

- Your iPhone stays with the Mac you paired it with. Opening NativeAgent on another Mac signed in to the same Apple Account no longer takes it over; to move it, choose Use my iPhone with this Mac in Connectors → iPhone on that Mac.
- Replies stream as formatted text and settle in place, including turns started from another door.
- A quiet Mac shows as here instead of unreachable, and a stale page explains itself and opens connection repair.
- History pages back past the newest 50 and opens whole conversations from the Mac.
- Approvals read in words; answer shared cards and send files from your phone.
- Activity opens on Approvals, Inbox, Memories to keep and Improvements; every Mac setting sits behind one Settings door.

Requires NativeAgent on a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.
