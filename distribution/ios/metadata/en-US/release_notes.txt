NativeAgent 0.4.11 refines navigation and makes synced activity clearer.

- All five tabs now use consistent outlined icons, including the selected tab.
- Activity waits for its own data before presenting its state.
- Freshness is reported separately for each group of synced information.
- Desk reports omitted information so incomplete snapshots are clear.

Requires NativeAgent on a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Update the Mac companion to 0.4.11 for the latest capabilities.
